Interview Tips & Resources

Before the Interview
  • Research the Company

    Learn about the company's mission, values, recent news, and projects.

  • Know Your Resume

    Be ready to discuss every point on your resume in detail.

  • Practice Common Questions

    Prepare answers for questions like "Tell me about yourself" and "Why do you want this job?"

  • Prepare Questions

    Ask thoughtful questions about the role, team, and company culture.

  • Get Rest

    Sleep well the night before your interview to be alert and focused.

During the Interview
  • Dress Professionally

    Choose appropriate attire that matches the company culture.

  • Make Eye Contact

    Maintain good eye contact to show confidence and engagement.

  • Listen Carefully

    Pay close attention to questions before answering them.

  • Speak Clearly

    Speak at a moderate pace and avoid using filler words like "um" and "uh".

  • Smile and Be Positive

    Show enthusiasm and maintain a positive attitude throughout.

After the Interview
  • Send a Thank You Email

    Follow up within 24 hours with a personalized thank you message.

  • Reflect on Performance

    Think about what went well and what could be improved.

  • Follow Up

    If you haven't heard back, follow up after the specified timeframe.

  • Stay Positive

    Continue applying to other positions while waiting for a response.

  • Learn and Improve

    Use feedback to improve your interview skills for future opportunities.

Common Interview Questions

Provide a brief summary of your background, experience, and career goals. Focus on relevant achievements.

Show genuine interest in the role and company. Mention specific reasons why you're excited about the opportunity.

Highlight 2-3 relevant strengths backed by examples. Relate them to the job requirements.

Choose a genuine weakness and discuss how you're working to improve it. Show self-awareness.
